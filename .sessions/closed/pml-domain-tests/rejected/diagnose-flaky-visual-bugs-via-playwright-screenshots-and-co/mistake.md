- **wrong:** Catalog carousel visually collapses when seeded with fewer than 4 sellable plans. In the e2e sandbox, after seeding the plan catalog fixture with only 3 sellable plans (Basic, Plus, Pro) plus 1 zero-price/non-sellable plan, the Catalog step's plan cards rendered correctly for a fraction of a second and then visually collapsed/shrank into a thin sliver within ~500ms-1.5s, leaving the page looking blank below the "Month-to-Month Plans" title. Root cause: PlanSelector (pml-harmony/lib/components/PlanSelector/PlanSelector.tsx) uses react-slick with a hardcoded defaultSettings responsive breakpoint (>=3200px) of slidesToShow: 4, initialSlide: 2, used whenever the numberOfCards prop isn't passed. src/pages/SignUp/steps/Catalog/Catalog.tsx never passes numberOfCards to <PlanSelector>, so it always uses defaultSettings. When fewer than 4 sellable plans are rendered (e.g. 3), react-slick's post-mount initialSlide repositioning (500ms CSS transition) computes slide width/track position assuming 4 slides exist, causing the whole track to collapse/shrink visually. Diagnosis method used (worth reusing for any future "flaky visual" complaints in this app): used Playwright itself, via the already-open e2e test helper page, to capture screenshots and page.evaluate() DOM/computed-style dumps of the button-to-card ancestor chain at t0 and t+1500ms, comparing element widths (they shrank from ~5-9px "sliver" ancestors toward 0px) to confirm this was a real CSS/layout collapse, not a test-timing artifact — rather than guessing or trying to attach external tooling.
- **status:** rejected
