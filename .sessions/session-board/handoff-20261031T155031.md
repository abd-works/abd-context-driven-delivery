## Rules the board follows

Every `.sessions/` folder is a ticket except `default/`, `closed/` (shown as Complete), and `_` folders (`_backlog/` is the Backlog column, `_archive/` is off the board). Three fixed stages; the board's practices give each stage its fidelities through each fidelity's `stage:`. A ticket starts in the earliest stage its log shows work in, and moves only by a `pull <stage>` line.
