# Context Gaps

A **context gap** is genuinely missing information — something the source material does not tell you and you cannot honestly infer. Not a to-do, not a nice-to-have.

Record gaps **inside the artifact** (not in chat) wherever makes the most sense: inline next to the node they concern, or in a `## Context Gaps` section at the bottom of the file.
