## Scope boundary

**In scope:** Spider-Man finds a supply (such as web-fluid cartridges or a suit repair kit), puts it in his cart, places the order, and the payment service charges for it.
**Out of scope:** delivery, returns and refunds, accounts and sign-in, and any other shopper. This is a demo map, kept to one epic on purpose.
