General Lee fired up on the back roads outside Hazzard, orange paint flashing through sheets of mud as Bo and Luke pointed him toward the county courthouse. Sheriff Rosco P. Coltrane was right on their tail — siren wailing, cruiser sliding in the ruts — but the Charger ate the muddy curves like they were paved.

Through creek beds and pine-lined bends, General Lee kept his wheels spinning and his horn blaring. Rosco took a bad line in a deep patch and spun sideways, throwing up a wall of brown spray while the Dukes shot past laughing. One final leap over a rain-swollen ditch, a four-wheel drift onto Main Street, and General Lee rolled up to the courthouse steps with mud on his fenders and victory in his engine.

> **Dodge Charger says:** "Yee-haw!
