# Recipe Guide

## Technique

Describe each step clearly: list mise en place first, then active cooking steps in order of execution. Use active voice. State temperatures in °F and timing in minutes. Each step should name exactly one action — "sauté the onions until translucent, about 5 minutes."

## Plating

Plate hot food on warm plates. Sauce before garnish. Leave negative space — less is more. Wipe the rim before serving.
