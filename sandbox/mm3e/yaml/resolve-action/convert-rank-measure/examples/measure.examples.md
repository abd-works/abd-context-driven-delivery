# Examples — Convert Rank Measure

From HeroesHandbook.md lines 336–389 and 399–808. Use the next higher listed rank when a measure falls between rows. Negative ranks halve. Add the measures, not the ranks.

| Example | Rank | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| strength lifts | mass 3 | 400 lbs. |
| ten-ton truck | mass 9 | 12 tons on the table; a 10-ton truck uses this next higher rank |
| strength throws | distance 1 | 60 feet |
| rank 4 distance | distance 4 | 500 feet |
| rank 6 distance | distance 6 | 1,800 feet |
| added distance | distance 7 | 2,300 feet, about half a mile |
| one hour | time 9 | 1 hour |
| one round | time 0 | 6 seconds |
| speed 14 over 30 miles | time -1 | 3 seconds |
| six miles | distance 11 | 8 miles on the table; 6 miles uses this next higher rank |
