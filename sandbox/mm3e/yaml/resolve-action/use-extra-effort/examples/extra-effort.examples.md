# Examples — Use Extra Effort

From HeroesHandbook.md lines 1426–1489. Extra effort is a free action once per turn. Fatigue arrives at the start of the next turn unless a hero point removes it.

| Benefit | Observable change | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| action | one more standard action | can be traded for a move or a free action |
| bonus | +2 on one check, or a +2 becomes +5 | also negates -2, or reduces -5 to -2 |
| power | +1 rank on one effect until the hero's next turn | permanent effects cannot increase |
| power stunt | an Alternate Effect until the end of the scene or its duration | permanent effects cannot be stunted |
| resistance | one immediate extra resistance check | fatigue waits while compelled or controlled |
| retry | another attempt at an effect that requires extra effort to retry | no other benefit |
| speed | +1 speed rank until the hero's next turn | — |
| strength | +1 Strength rank until the hero's next turn | — |
| fatigue | fatigued at the start of the next turn | hero point removes it |
| fatigued again | exhausted | — |
| exhausted again | incapacitated | — |
