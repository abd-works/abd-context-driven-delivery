# CDD Sketch — Vacation Manager

## What It Does
A vacation management app where users can track their vacations, invite others to join them, and get a curated itinerary of things to do at their destination.

## Core Capabilities
- Track vacations (destinations, dates, status)
- Invite others to a vacation
- Get a curated itinerary of activities, restaurants, and attractions for the destination
