# The board shows the practice catalogue's practices, and each ticket works with its session's own

The board sends every practice its tickets work through, with its name and colour, and each fidelity once with its practice (`Board.fidelities`), from a set of its own that the checkout's `Sessions` makes once with `PracticeCatalogue.create_all()` and holds as the catalog shows them (`Sessions.board_practices`, round 8, since `Board.load` makes a board for every call; round 7 deleted the catalogue's shared instances, so no reader can change another's practices), while each ticket's progress comes from its session's own instance of each practice (`session.practices`), which holds that session's folders, formats, and graph. We keep the two: what the board shows of a practice is the same for every session, and no single session's instances can stand for the board, which shows sessions that are closed or in the backlog as well as the open one. A stage asks whether a fidelity is one the board works through by its practice's class (`Board.works_through`), which is the same for the catalogue's instance and a session's. A fidelity's progress asks whether its session works on its practice through the fidelity itself (`fidelity.session_output.is_enabled`), never by looking the practice up again by its key.
