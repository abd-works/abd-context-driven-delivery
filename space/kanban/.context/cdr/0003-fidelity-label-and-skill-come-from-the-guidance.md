# A fidelity's label and its skill command come from the catalog, which the board sends

**Status:** done, round 7 (finding 4 of `round7-space-tools.md`). Round 6 recorded this as waiting on the harness; round 7 found the answers already public, so it is no longer a skip.

The board page used to cut a fidelity's label out of its slug (`PracticeClient.fidelityNameOf`, `story-map` from `stories-story-map`) and build the slash command of its skill (`skillOf`, `/practices-{practice}-{fidelity}`), which repeated how the installer names a skill folder. Both answers were already in the harness: the catalog's view of a fidelity gives its board label (`CatalogFidelity.label`, through `BoardLabel`), and the catalog's view of a practice names the skill folder the skill install writes for any of its guidance (`CatalogPractice.skill_of`, from `FileInstallation.relative_path`, the naming the installer writes the skill with).

The board holds one `CatalogPractice` per practice (`Board.catalogued`, which the checkout's `Sessions.board_practices` makes once for the process) and sends `Board.fidelities`: one `BoardFidelity` per fidelity, with the fidelity's slug, its practice's key, its `label`, and its `skill`. The page shows those two fields and works out neither; `fidelityNameOf` and `skillOf` are deleted. The label now follows the catalog's naming everywhere, so UX's `ia` tile reads `information-architecture`, as its catalog page and board ticket do.
