# Story Map Outline — Example

## Example shaping outline

(E) Manage Customer Orders
    * approx 18-22 total stories
    (E) Place New Order
        (S) Customer --> Browse Product Catalog
        (S) Customer --> Submit Order
        * approx 4-5 more stories (cart, address, delivery option, review)
    (E) Track Order Status
        * approx 3-4 more stories (pending, shipped, delivered notifications)
    (E) Cancel Order
        (S) Customer --> Request Order Cancellation
        * approx 2-3 more stories (refund, partial cancel, policy checks)

## What to notice

- **Outline, not complete** — most sub-epics have estimates instead of full story lists
- **Confirming stories** — enough named stories to prove each epic is real (2+ per epic)
- **Estimate lines** — `* approx N-M …` on every epic and sub-epic
- Epic and sub-epic names are **verb–noun**; actor goes before `-->`, not in the story name
