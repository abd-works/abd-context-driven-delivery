# The prompt notifier's name list and toast format move to the harness objects that own them, in three steps

The prompt notifier keeps its own list of practice, fidelity, rule, and action names (`PromptNotify.catalog`), works out a tool's toast and published names from `tool.callable`, its classes, and `toolset.slug`, and `ToastFile` takes apart the text `rules_toast` builds in `harness/hooks/hook_server.py`. Each of those answers belongs to an object in `harness`, which this module may not change beyond a caller: the practice catalogue and the action catalogue know their names, *AgentTool* knows its toast and its published names, and the hook server builds the rules toast. The decided order is: (1) `harness/guidance` gives *PracticeCatalogue* and *ActionCatalogue* the name a text mentions, and `harness/agent_tools` gives *AgentTool* its `toast` and `mcp_names`; (2) `harness/hooks` makes a toast a value (its source and the guidance it names) that joins itself into its text, kept beside `rules_toast`; (3) this notifier then deletes `_catalog`, its walk over toolsets for toasts, and `ToastFile`'s parsing, and asks those objects. The notifier loads every toolset of the running checkout itself (`echo_toolsets`) rather than taking the hook server's: the hook server's catalog holds only the toolsets whose hooks run, and a toast can sit on any toolset's MCP tool.
